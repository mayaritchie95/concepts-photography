Meet Karee

Her love of life and ability to capture the spirit of a person or a moment is what makes her unique.

With a respect for conventional photography, she tries to keep her work as honest as possible. Above all, Karee is an energetic, spirited person who likes to capture the same in others and does so with panache and a style all her own.
