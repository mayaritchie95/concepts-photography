Privacy Policy.

Last updated: September 2026

CONCEPTS Photography & Design Inc. (“we,” “us,” or “the Company”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your choices. We handle personal information in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation.

Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us. When you submit an inquiry through our contact form or email us directly, we may collect:

  • Your name;
  • Your email address and phone number;
  • Details about the photography services you are interested in;
  • Any other information you include in your message.

Our contact form works by opening your own email application; the information you enter is sent to us as an email. We do not automatically collect this data on a server through the form itself.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about services;
  • Prepare quotes, schedule sessions, and fulfill photography contracts;
  • Keep records required for our business and tax obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Photographs and image rights

Photographs taken as part of a commissioned session are handled according to the terms of your individual photography agreement. We will not publish or use images of you for marketing or portfolio purposes without your consent, as set out in that agreement.

Third-party services

This website may be hosted and supported by third-party providers, and our digital marketing is managed on our behalf by a marketing agency. These providers may process limited technical information (such as server logs) in the course of operating the site. Any such providers are expected to protect your information consistent with this policy and applicable law.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand how visitors use the site and to improve it. You can set your browser to refuse cookies; some features may not function as intended if you do.

How we protect your information

We take reasonable steps to safeguard the personal information in our custody against loss, theft, and unauthorized access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law, after which it is securely deleted or destroyed.

Your rights

Subject to legal limits, you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
