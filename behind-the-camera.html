Behind the Camera

Karee Davidson.

Image, Conner Holmes

Karee Davidson is a Canadian portrait photographer who divides her time between Calgary, Alberta and Saskatoon, Saskatchewan. She has been photographing people and places for over 35 years. Her love of life and ability to capture the spirit of a person or a moment is what makes her unique in the Canadian photographic community.

Karee graduated from the Visual Arts Program at the Alberta College of Art & Design in 1994. Since then she has built a, well established, trusted photography business. With a respect for conventional photography, she tries to keep her work as honest as possible.

Karee works consistently to improve her lighting skills and range of ability. Her family and new grandson Henrik allow her to experiment and are among her favourite informal subjects.

Above all, Karee is an energetic, spirited person who likes to capture the same in others and does so with panache and a style all her own.
