Honest photography

Kept as honest as possible.

With a respect for conventional photography, Karee tries to keep her work as honest as possible. She works consistently to improve her lighting skills and range of ability.

Above all, Karee is an energetic, spirited person who likes to capture the same in others and does so with panache and a style all her own.
