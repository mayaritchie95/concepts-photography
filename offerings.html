The difference

What makes me and my work different?

To me, photography is about more than good exposure; that part goes without saying. These are not snapshots. My goal is to illustrate something about every single person I photograph. My only request is that you relax, be yourself and trust that you can leave the rest up to me.

A session with me is fun! I have never had an individual leave my studio not feeling great about their experience. I am proud to say that in 2027 my business turns 25 and that I’ve been photographing people for over 35 years! Everyone knows repetition is the key to mastering your craft.

Here are a few things that come with my experience and make working with me different from other photographers.

  • Skill. You can relax; you’re in good hands. Your comfort is paramount. It allows me to do what I do and yield the best possible result.
  • Reliability. I promise not to disappear. Scheduling, timeline / delivery of files, and professionalism is important to me.
  • A fun environment with good coffee.
